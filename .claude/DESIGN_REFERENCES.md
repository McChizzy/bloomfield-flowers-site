# Bloomfield Design References

Use these as inspiration when improving Bloomfield Flowers or building adjacent Bloomfield sites. Do not copy their look wholesale; adapt the interaction patterns to a premium flower-shopping experience.

## Reference Sites

- https://beautifului.dev
- https://beui.dev
- https://rareui.com
- https://transitions.dev
- https://ui.shadcn.com

## What To Borrow

- Shadcn-style component discipline: consistent buttons, inputs, badges, tabs, sheets, dialogs, empty states, and form feedback.
- Transitions.dev-style motion: short, purposeful transitions for card resizing, menu opening, checkout state changes, number changes, and notifications.
- beUI-style interactive polish: expandable controls, morphing modal/sheet patterns, tooltip behavior, animated tabs, toast stacks, and subtle action swaps.
- Rare UI-style standout moments: one or two memorable hero/product interactions, not a full page of effects.
- Beautiful UI-style status clarity: compact status rows, progress chips, context cards, and clear confirmation/error states.

## Bloomfield Adoption Ideas

- Product cards: add quick-view behavior, price/location badges, smoother add-to-cart confirmation, and richer hover/focus states.
- Checkout: use clearer step states, animated order summary updates, discount-code status, delivery-fee confirmation, and mobile bottom-sheet summary.
- Navigation: refine mobile menu as a smooth drawer or bottom sheet, with active route indicators.
- Campaign pages: use segmented tabs for cities/occasions and animated reveal sections for campaign details.
- Trust/social proof: turn reviews and delivery proof into compact cards with badges, source labels, and subtle carousel motion.
- Admin/internal tools: use Beautiful UI-style task rows, records tables, filters, status chips, and recommendation cards.

## Guardrails

- Keep Bloomfield warm, floral, premium, and sales-focused.
- Avoid heavy dark developer-tool styling for the customer-facing flower site.
- Use motion to clarify action and state changes, not as decoration.
- Keep image quality and bouquet visibility higher priority than UI effects.
- Validate mobile first; most customers likely arrive from Instagram/WhatsApp.
