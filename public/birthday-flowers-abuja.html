How do I order birthday flowers for same-day delivery in Abuja?

Visit our shop, add your chosen bouquet to cart, select your Abuja delivery area, and complete checkout before 2pm. Your flowers will be delivered the same day.

Yes — there's a card message field on the checkout form. We'll include a handwritten note with your bouquet at no extra charge.

Delivery fees range from ₦3,500 (Diplomatic Zone, King's Court, Ministers Hill) to ₦10,000 (Mararaba, Masaka) depending on your area: Wuse and Wuse 2 are ₦5,000, Garki, Jabi and Mabushi ₦6,000, Maitama, Asokoro, Gwarinpa and Lugbe ₦7,000, Apo ₦8,000 and Kubwa ₦9,000.
