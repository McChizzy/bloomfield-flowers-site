Frequently Asked Questions

Do you deliver flowers same-day in Abuja?

Yes — orders confirmed and paid before 2pm (Monday to Saturday) qualify for same-day delivery. Sunday delivery is available between 1pm and 4pm for orders placed before noon.

Are Abuja prices different from Lagos prices?

Yes. Because most of our flowers are sourced closer to Lagos, Abuja pricing reflects the higher-end of each range. Select Abuja as your city in the cart and prices will update automatically.

Can I send flowers to a government office or hotel in Abuja?

Absolutely. We deliver to residences, hotels, offices, and event venues across Abuja. Add any entry instructions in the delivery notes at checkout.

Can I order a custom bouquet for delivery in Abuja?

Yes. Use our Custom Orders form to describe exactly what you want — flower type, colour palette, size, and occasion. We'll put together a bespoke arrangement and confirm availability before your order is finalised.
