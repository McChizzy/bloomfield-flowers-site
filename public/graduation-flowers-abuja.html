Frequently Asked Questions

Can I order graduation flowers for delivery to a venue or campus in Abuja?

Yes. We deliver to homes, event venues, campuses, and hotels across Abuja. Include the delivery address and any access notes at checkout and we'll handle the rest.

What size bouquet is best for graduation photos?

For graduation photos, we recommend The Radiant Garden or the BFF Collection — both are full, structured arrangements that look incredible in photos. The Seraphina is a great choice for a more portable, elegant option.

Can I get flowers in specific colours for a graduation?

Absolutely. Use our Custom Orders form to request a specific colour palette — whether it's your school's colours or a personal preference. We'll build the arrangement to match.

How far in advance should I order graduation flowers in Abuja?

For same-day delivery, order before 2pm on the day. For larger or custom arrangements, 2–3 days' notice is recommended to guarantee availability.
